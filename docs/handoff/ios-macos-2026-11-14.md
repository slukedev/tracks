# iPhone and Mac work to run locally (2026-11-14)

Two tasks for a session that has Xcode. Paste each brief into Claude Code on your Mac, in
this repo, on top of `feat/android-m3-expressive`. They touch different files and can run
in parallel. Android is handled separately and isn't part of either.

Decisions behind them (also in Notion memory, Tracks):

- Tracks only records songs played in Tracks itself, on every platform.
- Keep only the Last.fm history import. Remove the Motif import and Play Back.
- The Last.fm import has no confirmation pop-up; a short inline explanation replaces it.
- Settings copy should be much shorter.
- Getting music you don't own (Octo, Lidarr) shows only when a configured server allows
  it, and is prominent then, including on artist and album pages.

### Survey of what records outside plays today

Everything goes through one pipeline: `CaptureService` (Apps/Shared/Capture/CaptureService.swift)
owns a `MergedNowPlayingSource` and feeds `CaptureCoordinator.handle(_:)`
(TracksCore/Sources/TracksCore/Capture/CaptureCoordinator.swift). The coordinator doesn't know
which player an observation came from; outside and own plays differ only in which sources go
into the merged source (CaptureService.swift around lines 99 to 111) and the fallback order in
`currentObservation()` (around lines 601 to 624).

Outside capture, iPhone:
- `SystemMusicPlayerSource` (TracksCore/Sources/TracksMusic/Capture/SystemMusicPlayerSource.swift):
  the Music app, Control Center, Siri, CarPlay's Music app. Also the fallback in
  `currentObservation()`.
- Recently Played import: `CaptureService.importRecentlyPlayed()` with `RecentlyPlayedSource`,
  `TracksStore.importPlayedSongs` (TracksStore.swift around line 565) and `HistoryImport`.
  Rows get `CaptureKind.imported`. Runs from `catchUp()` (launch, Music access granted, iOS
  RootView, Background App Refresh, the Mac app, the Mac Summary "fill in" button).
- Background App Refresh (Apps/iOS/App/BackgroundRefresh.swift, BackgroundRefreshAvailability.swift,
  Apps/iOS/Settings/BackgroundRefreshNotice.swift, the BGTask id in Info.plist): its capture
  job is `catchUp()`; it also drains the playlist, scrobbles and artwork.
- Save Current Radio Song intent and Save Radio Song control
  (Apps/Shared/Intents/SaveCurrentRadioSongIntent.swift, ControlIntents.swift,
  ControlIntents+App.swift, Widgets/Shared/TracksControls.swift, TracksShortcuts phrases).

Outside capture, Mac:
- `PlayerInfoSource` (TracksMusic/Capture/PlayerInfoSource.swift, the
  `com.apple.Music.playerInfo` notification, AppleScript via MusicScripting on ScriptingQueue),
  wrapped in `DeferringNowPlayingSource`.
- The 10 second Music.app poll, `CaptureService.startPolling()`. It also drives
  `isSomethingPlaying` for the status item and auto Play Back: re-home the status item on
  Tracks' own player state.
- The Recently Played import and `captureCurrentSong()` through PlayerCommands, as above.

What keeps recording Tracks' own plays (keep):
- `ApplicationMusicPlayerSource` (TracksMusic/Capture/ApplicationMusicPlayerSource.swift), driven by
  `MusicKitPlayerEngine` for the Play tab, CarPlay and Siri's Play in Tracks; `TracksPlayerContext`
  states `isStation` and `stationName`.
- `PushedNowPlayingSource.yourMusic`, fed by `LocalPlayerEngine` for files and servers.
- Keep `currentObservation()` and `scheduleRecheck`; drop only the outside fallback.

Lost or changed when outside capture goes:
- Recently Played recovery (`importsRecentlyPlayed`, `recentlyPlayedAnchor`, HistorySettingsPage).
- `scrobblesImported` (LastFMSettingsPage around lines 179 to 202), pause/resume anchoring
  (`anchorOwedKey`), `passedOverSongs`/`notePassedOver` in PlayerModel, forgotten-song import grace.
- `RadioHeuristic`, `QueueEntryIdentifier`, `PlayerInfoKeys`, the "Treat Everything as Radio"

- Heard on Radio playlist and excluded stations still work for stations played in Tracks; trim copy.
- Play Back: Apps/Shared/Playback/PlatformPlaybackService.swift, TracksMusic/Playback/MusicAppPlaybackService.swift,
  MusicLibraryPlayback, PlaybackController, `autoPlayBack`/`considerAutoPlayback`, the Play Back

Study how Your Music decides what you're missing and how it detects whether a server can fetch:

Make "things to get" prominent wherever it's natural, but only when a configured server allows
fetching (an Octo-capable Subsonic server, or Lidarr). With no such server, those sections and
Get buttons don't appear at all.
- Your Music: a clear "Get more" area of suggested songs and albums you don't own, picked from
  your listening (artists you play most, similar artists), with Get, progress and done states,
  and context menus.
- Artist pages (history artist pages, Apple Music artist pages in Play, and Your Music or server
  artist pages): that artist's albums and songs you don't own yet, marked owned, partly owned or
  missing, with Get.
- Album pages: the missing tracks of albums you partly own, with Get for the rest.
- Search: results you don't own can be fetched from search when the server allows.
- The Downloads page is easy to reach from these places and shows what's being fetched.

Keep Apple Music playback paths working; this is about your own server. Note logic that could
move into the shared Rust engine later (suggestion ranking, availability, a Lidarr client);
Android is getting a Lidarr client and catalog search, possibly in the engine.
